# On-brand copy for hunter-lease

Audience: Russian-speaking SoCal buyers (lease AND finance; many prefer finance/ownership), plus
newcomers with thin US credit. Hard mechanics first: bilingual EN + RU, and **no em-dashes, ever**.

## The one idea: relief = leverage / status, never rescue
Their #1 fear is **обман** — being deceived and not even knowing it. It is a wound to dignity and
competence, not just money. So every promise must make the reader the smartest, best-armed person,
NEVER a victim being protected. Tell a proud buyer "you'll lose / don't be a sucker" and they go to
the dealer to prove you wrong.

## Top fears -> our weapon (what to answer)
1. Обман (numbers switched / fees added after the handshake) -> price + every fee fixed in advance.
2. Hidden fees / forced add-ons -> itemized line-by-line; and name OUR own deposit loudly.
3. No US credit history -> "no credit is a clean slate, not bad credit"; free soft-pull first.
4. Деньги вперёд (money before certainty) -> the deposit is a refundable lock, "not a payment for a
   promise"; certainty first, money second.
5. Monthly-payment shell game / MF markup -> show the whole math; lead with price, not "$X/mo".
6. Overpaying like a "лох" -> "wholesale, not retail, like для своих" — you already won.
7. Charged more for being an immigrant -> "one wholesale number for everyone; your accent decides nothing".
8. Leasing = renting / "ничего не остаётся" -> offer finance as a FIRST-CLASS equal, never hard-sell lease.

## DO say
- A verifiable MECHANISM, not adjectives: "dealers dump cars to hit quotas, we catch those deals".
- "The price you see is the price you pay. Out-the-door, locked." (the testable anti-обман promise)
- "Know your payment up front, with no hit to your credit." (soft-pull first)
- Pair "we speak your language" with checkable proof: the CA license badge, title in the buyer's name.
- Understated, numbers-first. This audience trusts checkable figures over hype.

## DON'T say (these break trust with this audience)
- "without us you'll be cheated / you'll lose to the dealer / don't be a sucker" — insults competence.
- Pity tone: "we'll help even you / bad credit? we'll help" — re-wounds a once-successful newcomer.
- Lead with "$X/mo" as the hook — that is the four-square they were warned about.
- Hype superlatives ("best prices!", "100% transparent!", exclamation marks) — pattern-matches to ads
  they discount.
- "no SSN / ITIN / up to 99% approval" — hunter.lease REQUIRES SSN; this mis-sets expectations.
- "we're your fellow Russians, trust us" as the core pitch alone — it is the co-ethnic scammer's line;
  always back it with verifiable facts.
- Pressure/urgency timers, fabricated market averages, fake "verified 2h ago", invented VINs.

## Mechanics
- Bilingual: every string needs EN + RU (RU natural, not a literal translation).
- No em-dashes (—). Use commas, colons, periods, or " - ".
- Brand assets to keep: the CA license badge near the hero; "wholesale, not retail"; the calm,
  numbers-first tone. Hero copy lives in `src/translations.ts` (`hero` block, EN ~93 / RU ~1966) and
  renders in `src/App.tsx`.

Full research (81 fears, sources, sample quotes) is in the founder's auto-memory hunter_lease_strategy.
